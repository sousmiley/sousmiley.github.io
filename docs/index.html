About Me


I'm a part-time coder and full-time cryptanalyst. I like designing things, especially software, ciphers, and puzzles.

I have an extensive background in design and marketing through my work as a Senior Yearbook Editor, as well as Web Designer and Social Media Director for the PRSJC. As the former Director of Events for SFU Team Phantom, I enjoy organizing experiences that bring people together and build a strong sense of community.
